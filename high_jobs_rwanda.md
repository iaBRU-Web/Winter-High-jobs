# HIGH JOBS RWANDA — PRESENTATION SPEECH

**Presented by: MUGISHA DAVID**

Good morning, everyone.

What if finding employment in Rwanda became easier, faster, and smarter?

That is the vision behind **HIGH JOBS RWANDA**.

**The problems HIGH JOBS RWANDA aims to solve are:**

Difficulty finding job opportunities, poor connections between employers and job seekers, limited access to employment information, and the challenge of matching people's skills with available jobs.

**Our proposed solutions are:**

Building a digital platform that brings job seekers and employers together, making opportunities easier to discover and recruitment more accessible.

**What makes our platform innovative?**

We aim to use artificial intelligence to make job searching smarter, help people discover relevant opportunities, and improve the connection between skills and employment.

**The challenges we expect to face are:**

Attracting employers, building user trust, reaching more people, maintaining the platform, and covering operating costs.

**How do we plan to overcome these challenges?**

Through partnerships with companies, institutions, sponsors, and continuous investment in technology.

**Our planned sources of income are:**

Employer services, optional premium features, recruitment partnerships, and other business services.

**The impact we want to create is:**

Helping Rwandans discover employment opportunities, helping businesses find talent, and contributing to Rwanda's digital economy.

Our bigger vision is to make sure that talent gets the opportunity to shine, regardless of how difficult finding employment may be.

**HIGH JOBS RWANDA — Connecting Talent to Opportunity.**

Thank you.
