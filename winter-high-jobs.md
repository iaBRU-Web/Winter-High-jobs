# HIGH JOBS RWANDA + WINTER-FROST

## COMBINED PROJECT PRESENTATION SPEECH

**Presented by: INEZA AIME BRUNO And MUGISHA DAVID**

Good morning, everyone.

What if finding a job was not just about searching, but also having artificial intelligence guide you along the way?

That is the vision behind combining **HIGH JOBS RWANDA and WINTER-FROST**.

**The problems this combination aims to solve are:**

Difficulty finding suitable jobs, poor connections between employers and job seekers, limited career guidance, and the challenges people face when using technology to find employment.

**Our combined solution is:**

HIGH JOBS RWANDA provides the platform connecting job seekers with employers, while WINTER-FROST powers its AI assistant, helping make the employment experience smarter and more interactive.

**How do the two projects work together?**

WINTER-FROST connects to HIGH JOBS RWANDA through an API. This allows the platform's AI assistant to process users' questions and provide helpful responses.

For example, a job seeker could ask for career guidance or help understanding an employment opportunity, while employers could use AI assistance when navigating the platform.

**The challenges we expect to face are:**

Computing costs, API reliability, infrastructure, data protection, attracting employers, and continuously improving the AI assistant.

**How do we plan to overcome these challenges?**

Through testing, better infrastructure, research, partnerships, investment, and continuous development.

**Our funding and income opportunities include:**

Technology investors, innovation grants, sponsorships, employer services, premium platform features, and paid AI services.

**The impact we want to create is:**

Making job opportunities easier to access, helping employers connect with talent, supporting job seekers with AI assistance, and encouraging Rwandan technology innovation.

Together, these projects represent more than a job platform and an AI model. They represent an opportunity to combine local technology to solve real problems in Rwanda.

I believe technology should not only make life easier; it should create opportunities for people.

**HIGH JOBS RWANDA connects talent to opportunity, powered by WINTER-FROST AI.**

Thank you.
