# WINTER-FROST — PRESENTATION SPEECH
**Presented by INEZA AIME BRUNO**

Good morning, everyone.

What if Rwanda could develop its own artificial intelligence, compete in the global AI race, and become a creator of AI technology instead of depending only on foreign solutions?

That is the vision behind **WINTER-FROST**.

**The problems WINTER-FROST aims to solve are:**

Expensive AI services, difficult AI integration, limited computing resources, and a lack of locally developed AI solutions.

Many Rwandans depend on AI models developed in other countries, often accessing them through API keys. But why should we only depend on existing technology when we can develop our own and empower more Rwandans to enter the AI industry?

**Our proposed solutions are:**

Developing a Rwandan AI model, providing an accessible API for developers, and creating opportunities for young people and businesses to build their own AI-powered applications.

**How does WINTER-FROST work?**

It has three main components: an AI model, an inference gateway, and a developer API console. Together, they allow developers to connect our AI to their websites and applications.

**The challenges we have faced are:**

High computing costs, limited resources, infrastructure difficulties, and improving model performance.

**How do we plan to overcome these challenges?**

Through investment, grants, sponsorships, research, and partnerships.

**Our planned sources of income are:**

Paid API access, business subscriptions, and custom AI integration services.

**The impact we want to create is:**

Supporting students, businesses, and developers while helping Rwanda become an active participant in the global AI race.

I believe Rwanda should not only use tomorrow's technology. **We should help create it.**

**WINTER-FROST: Rwandan Innovation. Local Intelligence. Solutions for the Future.**

Thank you.
